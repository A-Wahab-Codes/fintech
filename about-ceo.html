About the CEO

Leading Edge-Tech.ng Bank with a customer-first digital vision.

Oke Abdulwahab Adeshina is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Edge-Tech.ng Bank. His leadership focuses on secure digital banking, reliable customer service, and practical financial access for Nigerians.

Profile

A leader focused on trust, access, and innovation.

As CEO, Oke Abdulwahab Adeshina guides Edge-Tech.ng Bank toward simple, secure, and accessible banking services. His work is centered on helping customers manage money with confidence, whether they are saving, transferring funds, applying for support, or connecting with customer care.

Company Direction

The company, goal, aim, and ambition behind Edge-Tech.

Edge-Tech.ng Bank is focused on building simple digital banking services that help Nigerians access money tools with more confidence, speed, and trust.

About the Company

Edge-Tech.ng Bank is a digital banking company created to make financial services easier for individuals, families, and businesses. The company focuses on secure accounts, quick transfers, savings, loans, customer care, and digital access through web and USSD channels.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make banking feel simple and dependable. Edge-Tech wants customers to open accounts, move money, save, and get support without unnecessary stress, long delays, or confusing processes.

Our Aim

Our aim is to support financial inclusion by giving more Nigerians access to practical banking tools. We are building services that work for everyday users, small businesses, and growing communities.

Our Ambition

Our ambition is to become a trusted Nigerian fintech brand known for innovation, strong customer service, secure systems, and banking solutions that help people work, grow, and achieve more.
